For years the only catalogue of the collection was in my head, with a few notebooks to back it up. This site is my attempt to change that: every piece numbered, photographed and described, in one place.

It's also a way to share. Many of the most useful things I've learned came from other collectors, so if you recognise something here, or know more about it than I do, I'd be glad to hear from you.
