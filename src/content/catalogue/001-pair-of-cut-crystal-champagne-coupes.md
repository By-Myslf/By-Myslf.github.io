A matched pair of shallow coupes on tall, slender stems. The lower part of each bowl and the edge of the foot are cut with fine radiating facets that catch the light when the glass is filled.

The broad, open coupe was the fashionable way to serve champagne until the narrow flute took over in the mid-20th century.
