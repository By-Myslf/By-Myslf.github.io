Four tiles, framed together, showing a river landscape with a windmill and a small sailing boat. The corner motifs, known as ox-heads, are typical of mid-18th-century Delft production.

The first piece in the collection, bought at a country auction in a box of mixed tiles. Three more from the same set turned up years later.
