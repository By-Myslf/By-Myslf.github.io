Most European silver carries a small group of stamped marks. Together they work like a passport for the object.

## The four common marks

1. **The standard mark** shows the purity of the silver, such as 925 or 800.
2. **The town mark** shows where the piece was tested.
3. **The date letter**, used in some countries, shows the year.
4. **The maker's mark**, usually initials, identifies the workshop.

## Where to start

A good reference book for the country you collect most is worth more than any website. Use a loupe, good daylight, and patience: worn marks can take a long time to read.
