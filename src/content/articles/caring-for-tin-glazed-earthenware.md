Tin-glazed earthenware has a soft body under a hard, glassy glaze. Wherever the glaze is chipped, the body beneath can absorb water and dirt.

## Display

Keep pieces away from direct sunlight and radiators, and use plate stands with padded hooks rather than metal wire hangers.

## Cleaning

Dust with a soft brush. If a piece needs more than that, ask a conservator before using water: old repairs can be dissolved by it.
