A morning at the flea market

14 September 2026, by Zoltán Borics

Sample post: replace this with your own writing.

The best stalls are always the ones that look least promising. This morning it was a folding table covered in kitchen drawers’ worth of cutlery, and somewhere near the bottom, a small silver spoon with a mark I didn’t recognise.

What I’m looking into

The mark is a crowned letter inside a shield. I’ve sent photographs to a friend who knows far more about silver than I do, and I’ll write again when I hear back.

The pleasure of collecting is mostly in not knowing yet.

Until then, it sits on my desk, polished just enough to read the mark and no more.
