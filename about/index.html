About the collection

This is where you tell visitors who you are and how the collection began. Write it the way you would explain it to a guest standing in your living room.

A few things people usually want to know: what first drew you to antiques, what ties the collection together, and how you choose a piece. Mention a favourite object or two, and link to it in the catalogue.

You might also say whether you welcome questions from other collectors, researchers or dealers, and whether you ever lend pieces to exhibitions.

If you would like to know more about any piece, write to me.
