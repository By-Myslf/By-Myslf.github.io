About this piece

Over the years, Moët & Chandon Dom Pérignon has released a variety of special editions and luxurious accessories to underline the brand’s commitment to exclusivity and refinement. This particular shield-shaped porcelain ashtray is a stunning example of that legacy – a refined, stylish addition to any collector’s table or cigar lounge.

Known for their creative and elegant designs, Moët & Chandon Dom Pérignon accessories always stand out, and this shield-shaped ashtray is no exception – it’s a statement piece that sparks conversation.

A beautifully preserved vintage item in outstanding condition.
