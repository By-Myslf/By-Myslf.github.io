Louis Roederer – The Elegance of an Independent Champagne House

Founded in 1776, Louis Roederer is one of the last great independent, family-owned champagne houses. Its reputation is built on exceptional quality, meticulously selected vineyards, and generations of expertise.

Cristal – The Iconic Prestige Cuvée

These glasses are perfectly suited for enjoying the house’s most famous creation: Cristal. First crafted in 1876 for Tsar Alexander II of Russia, Cristal has since become one of the world’s most sought-after and exclusive champagnes. Its crystalline purity, refined structure, and unmatched harmony have made it a legend.

This glass set is not only functional, but also embodies a piece of Louis Roederer’s tradition and the timeless heritage of Cristal.
