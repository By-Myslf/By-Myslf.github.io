About this piece

The glasses and champagne cooler reflect Bollinger’s distinctive brand identity and are suitable for champagne service, home entertaining or inclusion in a private collection of champagne accessories.

Bollinger Elisabeth Champagne Glasses

The six long-stemmed champagne glasses were manufactured by the French glassmaker Lehmann for Champagne Bollinger.

The glasses feature tulip-shaped bowls that taper slightly towards the rim. This shape allows the champagne’s aromas to develop while helping to concentrate them during tasting.

A subtle fill line is visible on the bowl of each glass, while the bases are decorated with the white Bollinger logo.

The clear, thin-walled crystal glass, slender stem and wide, flat base create a balanced and understated appearance.

Glass Specifications:

The original packaging carries the designation Verre Bollinger – 44 cl by Lehmann.

The Elisabeth Name and the Story of Lily Bollinger

The Elisabeth name recalls Elisabeth „Lily” Bollinger, one of the most influential figures in the history of the Champagne House.

Elisabeth Law de Lauriston-Boubers (1899–1977) married Jacques Bollinger in 1923. Following her husband’s death in 1941, she took over the management of the family business, leading the Champagne House until 1971.

During her three decades of leadership, she played an important role in expanding Bollinger’s international reputation, particularly in Great Britain and the United States.

Throughout her career, she remained committed to preserving traditional champagne-making methods and maintaining Bollinger’s distinctive house style.

She was also closely associated with the introduction of Bollinger R.D. Champagne. R.D. stands for Récemment Dégorgé (Recently Disgorged), referring to vintage champagne that undergoes extended ageing on its lees before being disgorged relatively shortly before release.

Lily Bollinger became a well-known personality in champagne culture. Numerous stories and quotations are associated with her, including her famous humorous remarks about enjoying champagne on different occasions.

The Elisabeth glass name is associated with Lily Bollinger, although the official Elisabeth model designation is not confirmed on the original packaging of the 44 cl version presented here.

Bollinger Champagne Cooler – Eric Berthès Design

The second item in this collection is a Bollinger champagne cooler designed by Eric Berthès, made from transparent acrylic with red decorative side panels.

The cooler features thick transparent walls, a curved upper rim and two red vertical panels bearing the white BOLLINGER lettering.

Its transparent construction allows the champagne bottle and the ice inside to remain visible. The wide opening makes it easy to insert and remove the bottle.

The base of the cooler features the signature of designer Eric Berthès, the CHAMPAGNE BOLLINGER branding and the word PATENTED.

Eric Berthès is a French designer who has created several champagne coolers and bar accessories for Bollinger. His designs combine practical functionality with the Champagne House’s recognisable visual identity.

When filled with ice and water, the cooler can be used to chill and serve one champagne bottle.

It is suitable for use in bars, restaurants, at events or in private homes. It can also be displayed as a decorative or collectible object.
