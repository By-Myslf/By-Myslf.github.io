About this piece

Armand de Brignac, also known as „Ace of Spades”, is a well-known premium champagne brand. The design of this ice bucket reflects the brand’s distinctive gold and black colour scheme.

The front features the large, black Ace of Spades emblem with an engraved-effect finish, accompanied by the „ARMAND DE BRIGNAC CHAMPAGNE” lettering beneath it.

Material and Design

The ice bucket is constructed from double-walled stainless steel with a high-gloss, mirror-polished gold-coloured finish.

Its wide, flared shape provides a spacious interior, allowing multiple champagne bottles to be placed inside and chilled simultaneously with ice.

The double-walled construction helps reduce the influence of external temperatures, contributing to longer-lasting cooling of the ice and champagne bottles.

The exterior features a reflective gold-coloured surface, while the interior has a smooth metal finish that is easy to clean.

Capacity and Use

The spacious interior can accommodate up to 8 standard 750 ml champagne bottles or 4 Magnum 1.5-litre bottles, together with sufficient ice for chilling.

Its large capacity makes it particularly suitable for occasions where several bottles need to be chilled and served at the same time.

Suitable for private gatherings, receptions, weddings, corporate events, bars, restaurants, hotels, and other hospitality venues.

Thanks to its distinctive branding and gold-coloured finish, it can also be used as a decorative display piece or as part of a champagne-related collection.

Condition and Packaging

The ice bucket is in brand-new, never-used condition, free from scratches and dents.

Both the exterior gold-coloured coating and the interior metal surface are in excellent, undamaged condition.

The original factory protective packaging is included.
