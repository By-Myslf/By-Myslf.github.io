About this piece

Piper-Heidsieck XL Champagne Cooler – Glossy Red & Matte Gold Ice Bucket for 6 Bottles

Designed to impress, this oversized cooler is both a functional serving piece and a bold decorative statement. Its generous capacity comfortably accommodates up to six standard Champagne bottles, making it ideal for entertaining, celebrations, or professional hospitality settings.

Crafted from durable metal, the exterior features a vibrant, high-gloss red finish that instantly catches the eye, while the interior is elegantly contrasted with a refined matte gold coating—enhancing both its luxury appeal and presentation. The iconic Piper-Heidsieck logo is prominently displayed on both sides, reinforcing its authenticity and connection to one of Champagne’s most recognizable houses.

Whether used for chilling fine bottles or as a centerpiece at events, this cooler blends style, heritage, and practicality in one exceptional piece.

A rare and elegant addition for collectors, Champagne enthusiasts, or anyone looking to elevate their entertaining experience.

Scratch-free, never used.
