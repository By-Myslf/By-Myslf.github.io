About this piece

PIPER XXL ice basin – Illuminated battery portable – 8-bottle ice bath Piper-Heidsieck Champagne LED cooler – Made in France

This large bar decoration was made for Piper-Heidsieck champagnes.

8 0.75 size champagne bottles can easily fit in it.

It has a rechargeable battery, so it can be placed anywhere without a cable.

There is a small damage on one side edge. Not distracting.

It was otherwise never used.

It looks good during the day, but it really comes alive after dark!
