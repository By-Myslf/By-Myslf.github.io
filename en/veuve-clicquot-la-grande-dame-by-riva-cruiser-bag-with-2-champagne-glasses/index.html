About this piece

The black piano-lacquered finish is complemented by a mahogany and sycamore inlaid lid, reflecting the characteristic deck pattern of classic Riva yachts. Silver-coloured metal fittings, the handle and secure lock, together with leather details, give the bag a refined and cohesive appearance.

The interior is designed to hold one champagne bottle and two glasses. The set includes 2 Veuve Clicquot champagne glasses.

The Riva Cruiser can also be used for purposes other than carrying and storing champagne. Thanks to its size and design, it is suitable for storing documents, keys and smaller personal items, or it can be used as a travel case or beauty bag. It also works well as a decorative display piece in a bar, showcase or collection.

The sale includes only the Riva Cruiser Bag and 2 champagne glasses. The champagne bottle is not included.
