About this piece

Armand de Brignac, also known as Ace of Spades, is a well-known Champagne brand. Brut Gold is its best-known cuvée.

Dummy bottles are produced for bars, restaurants, shops, events and promotional displays, allowing the brand and bottle to be presented without using a real bottle of Champagne.

The set includes the gold-coloured display bottle, glossy black opening presentation box and padded carrying case. The bottle and box feature the raised Ace of Spades emblems.

Suitable for bar or restaurant decoration, display cabinets, home bars, photography, shop displays or Champagne collections.

Not for consumption – the bottle contains no Champagne.
