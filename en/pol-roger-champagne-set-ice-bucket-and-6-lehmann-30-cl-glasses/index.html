About this piece

Pol Roger Champagne Set – Ice Bucket + 6 Lehmann 30 cl Champagne Glasses

The white ice bucket features a modern, gently curved design with the black Champagne Pol Roger logo on the front. It is designed to hold and chill one standard 0.75 L champagne bottle with ice.

Ice bucket dimensions:

The set also includes 6 Lehmann Reims – France champagne glasses from the Collection Signature A. Lallement M5 series. These machine-made, lead-free crystal glasses each feature the white Pol Roger champagne logo on the foot.

Glass specifications:

  • Lead-free crystal
  • Machine-made
  • Dishwasher safe
  • Lehmann, Reims – France
  • Set of 6 in Lehmann box

Condition:

The complete set is new and has never been used. The glasses come with their Lehmann box.

1 Pol Roger champagne ice bucket + 6 Pol Roger × Lehmann 30 cl champagne glasses + Lehmann box.
