Dom Pérignon Champagne Stopper Designed by Martin Szekely

This stylish bottle stopper perfectly complements Dom Pérignon champagne ice buckets with its sleek black design, accentuated by a contrasting gold embossed maison emblem.

The best way to maintain the effervescence of an opened bottle is to ensure an airtight seal. This stopper features specially designed arms that lock securely, creating a vacuum to prevent the release of carbon dioxide and preserve the fizz.

The elegant design is tailored specifically to match the neck diameter of the iconic Dom Pérignon bottle, guaranteeing a perfect fit and seal.

The Dom Pérignon champagne stopper comes in its own DP dust bag, eliminating any need for additional packaging. It arrives ready to gift, making it a perfect champagne accessory for any occasion.
