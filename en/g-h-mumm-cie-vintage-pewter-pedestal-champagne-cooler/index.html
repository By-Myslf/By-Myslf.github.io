About this piece

G.H. Mumm & Cie Vintage Pewter Pedestal Champagne Cooler / Ice Basin

This champagne cooler reflects the elegance and outstanding quality associated with the prestigious G.H. Mumm & Cie maison.

Made of polished pewter, its timeless design makes it an ideal choice for exclusive events, fine dining establishments, or discerning collectors.

The cooler can accommodate 5–6 standard champagne bottles and, thanks to its cast pewter construction, keeps beverages chilled for an extended period.

Condition:

Excellent overall condition. This G.H. Mumm champagne cooler may never have been used and has been preserved in an almost completely scratch-free state, showing only minimal signs of handling.

G.H. MUMM & Cie – An icon of champagne culture.

The history of G:H: MUMM champagne house dates back to 1827, when the mumm family, of German origin, established the company in Reims, France. Having already been involved in the wine trade, the family committed itself to producing high-quality champagne in the heart of the Champagne region.

The brand’s iconic red ribbon (cordon rouge) was inspired by the French Legion of Honor and has become a universal symbol of luxury, prestige, and excellence. g.h. mumm champagnes owe their distinctive character and rich flavors to their pinot noir grape dominance, which gives them intensity and depth.

To this day, the house remains one of the most celebrated champagne brands in the world, frequently served at prestigious events, international sports competitions, and luxury gatherings.
