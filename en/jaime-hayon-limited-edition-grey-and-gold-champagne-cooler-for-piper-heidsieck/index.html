About this piece

Jaime Hayon’s limited-edition grey-gold dedicated metal champagne cooler for Piper-Heidsieck Champagne

Vintage French Jaime Hayon for Piper Heidsieck – Gray and Gold Metal Champagne Ice Cooler Bucket – RARE Collector’s speciality

Excellent condition, never used.

In 2006, renowned designer Jaime Hayon was commissioned by Piper-Heidsieck to create something unexpected that would elevate the brand’s prestige. The result was a bold, baroque-inspired collection – a fusion of extravagance and artistic form. This distinctive shape was later produced in various materials, including plastic and resin, with or without ornate baroque-style handles.

These champagne coolers are true design statements. When Jaime Hayon looked at a traditional ice bucket, he reimagined it through the lens of 18th-century baroque aesthetics, embedding historical elegance into a modern-day object. The result is a set of iconic design pieces that attract attention in any setting, especially when displayed as a pair.

Bottles that may be shown in the pictures are for decoration and to show dimensions.
