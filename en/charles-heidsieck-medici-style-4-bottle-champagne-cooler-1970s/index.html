About this piece

„Charles Heidsieck – Champagne – Reims, France”,

Rare 4-Bottle Vintage Medici-Style Champagne Cooler by Charles Heidsieck – 1970s, France

The cooler features beautifully balanced proportions and fine detailing. It stands 36 cm tall with a diameter of 34 cm, accommodating up to four bottles of champagne or wine when filled with ice.

One side is engraved with the inscription:

“Charles Heidsieck – Champagne – Reims, France”,

while the other side bears the signature of Charles Heidsieck himself, adding a unique and authentic historical touch to the piece.

It is in good vintage condition, structurally sound and free of any damage. Minor signs of use are visible primarily on the interior surface, consistent with age and typical usage—resulting from ice cubes and bottle handling over the decades.

Charles Heidsieck, often affectionately known as “Champagne Charlie”, was one of the most charismatic and visionary figures of 19th-century Champagne history. The maison he founded in Reims is still renowned today for its deep chalk cellars, extended aging, and elegant, expressive cuvées. This champagne cooler is not only a testament to the brand’s rich legacy but also a symbol of French art de vivre and refined celebration.

A true collector’s gem that brings history, sophistication, and a sense of grandeur to any setting.
