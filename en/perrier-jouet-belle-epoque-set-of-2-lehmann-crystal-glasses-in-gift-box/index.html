About this piece

The glasses are made from high-quality, machine-produced crystal and feature long, slender stems with tulip-shaped bowls. Each glass has a capacity of 9.6 US fl oz (285 ml).

The glasses are decorated with the signature Japanese anemone floral motif of the Perrier-Jouët Belle Époque collection, featuring white flowers, green leaves, and gold-coloured outlines.

The original floral design was created in 1902 by French Art Nouveau glass artist Émile Gallé for the Perrier-Jouët Champagne house. Since then, the anemone motif has become one of the brand’s most recognizable symbols.

The tulip-shaped bowls are designed for serving champagne. Their wider bodies and gently tapered rims help concentrate the wine’s aromas while allowing the bubbles to develop.

The set combines the recognizable Belle Époque floral design with Lehmann crystal glassmaking. Suitable for champagne tastings, special occasions, or as an addition to a collection of Perrier-Jouët Champagne accessories and memorabilia.
