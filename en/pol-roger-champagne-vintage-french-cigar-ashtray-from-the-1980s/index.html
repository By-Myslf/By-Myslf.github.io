About this piece

Measuring 18 × 18 cm, this ashtray was manufactured in France by Manufacture de Villenauxe, presumably for promotional or hospitality use by the Pol Roger Champagne house.

The ashtray features a square design with a deep navy blue, glossy glazed ceramic finish and gold-coloured Pol Roger Champagne lettering on the side. It has a solid construction with four cigar rests.

The underside of the ashtray bears the original French manufacturer’s mark:

Located in Villenauxe-la-Grande, France, Manufacture de Villenauxe had a long history of ceramic production. During the second half of the 20th century, the company manufactured promotional and hospitality-related ceramic items for various businesses and beverage brands.

This Pol Roger ashtray was most likely produced during the 1980s, when Champagne houses frequently used branded bar accessories and promotional items in restaurants, hotels and other hospitality establishments.

Pol Roger Champagne – Brand History and Collectible Interest

Founded in 1849 in Épernay, France, Pol Roger is a family-owned Champagne house known for its traditional Champagne production.

Vintage branded ashtrays are now collected by enthusiasts of Champagne memorabilia, vintage barware and cigar accessories.

Original examples from the 1980s and 1990s are generally found on the secondary market, with availability depending on their condition and design.

This ashtray is suitable for cigar use and can also be displayed in a home bar, cigar lounge or Champagne memorabilia collection.
