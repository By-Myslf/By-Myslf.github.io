About the Collection

The collection includes rare vintage and antique champagne coolers, exclusive champagne glasses, limited-edition bar accessories, unique promotional items, and creations by renowned designers. It offers a fascinating glimpse into the world of legendary champagne houses such as Dom Pérignon, Krug, Ruinart, Moët & Chandon, and Veuve Clicquot.

Every piece tells its own story of French champagne culture, luxury, craftsmanship, and timeless design.
