About this piece

Laurent-Perrier Large Champagne Ice Bucket – 8 Bottle Capacity

The bucket has a cylindrical shape with slightly tapered sides and a wide opening. The exterior features a polished, reflective stainless steel finish, while the interior has a brushed stainless steel surface.

The front is decorated with a rectangular gold-tone metal plaque featuring the Laurent-Perrier Champagne logo and the house’s founding year, 1812.

The large, adjustable metal carrying handle has a brown leather-covered grip with the Laurent-Perrier name embossed across its surface. The metal section of the handle also features Laurent-Perrier lettering, while the circular attachments on both sides are decorated with raised LP monograms.

The wide opening and large capacity allow several champagne bottles to be chilled simultaneously. Suitable for use in restaurants, hotels, bars, events, or as a branded display piece.
