About this piece

Abel Lepitre / Prince de Bourbon-Parme Champagne – Oak Champagne Bucket by Géraud Lafitte (Reims, early 1960s)

The maker’s stamp on the lower brass band reads:

Géraud Lafitte – Meilleur Ouvrier de France 1933, one of the highest distinctions in French craftsmanship, guaranteeing outstanding quality and expertise.

Buckets of this type were often used by the Abel Lepitre Champagne House in Reims for promotional events. The house maintained a close relationship with Prince André de Bourbon-Parme, for whom, in 1961, they created the prestigious Cuvée n°134, a blend of the finest vintages from the Côte des Blancs, representing the pinnacle of their range.

The bucket itself was produced in the early 1960s, while the associated Bourbon-Parme cuvée dates to 1961, placing this piece firmly within that important historical period.

More than just a functional item, this is a true piece of champagne history, reflecting the elegance of French aristocracy and the heritage of fine winemaking.

Condition is very good, well-preserved for its age and fully usable.

A rare and highly collectible piece, ideal for champagne enthusiasts, collectors, or as a refined decorative accent.
