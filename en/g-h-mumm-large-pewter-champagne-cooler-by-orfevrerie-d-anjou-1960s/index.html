About this piece

G.H. MUMM large pewter champagne cooler ice bowl – Orfèvrerie d’Anjou from the 1960s

On one side, the „G.H. Mumm & Cie” inscription is painted red, while on the other side, it appears unpainted. Originally, a red leather ribbon ran between the two ribbed borders, which may have worn away over time.

Upon request, we can restore this unique piece and have a leather artisan recreate the red leather ribbon to match its original appearance.

Style:

Material and Technique:

Condition:

In excellent condition for its age, with no significant scratches or deformations. Only minor patina and small wear marks from use are visible, enhancing its authentic vintage character.
