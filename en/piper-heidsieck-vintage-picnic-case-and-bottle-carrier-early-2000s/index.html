About this piece

Piper-Heidsieck Champagne Vintage Picnic Case / Bottle Carrier – Rare French Promotional Piece, from the early 2000s

The appearance of this piece fits well with the period when Piper-Heidsieck was significantly refreshing its visual identity and, alongside traditional Champagne packaging, was increasingly introducing design- and lifestyle-oriented promotional items.

In a 2001 French interview with Piper-Heidsieck’s marketing director (e-marketing.fr), Jean Poulallion explained that when he joined the company in April 1996, Piper bottles still carried a cream-coloured label. Richard Farnham subsequently developed the new packaging, and it was during this redesign that the distinctive “rouge Piper” shade was selected. In other words, the strong red brand identity now associated with Piper-Heidsieck emerged in 1996.

More precisely, Piper-Heidsieck’s red label was filed as a trademark on 16 October 1996, while the distinctive red colour itself was registered on 21 November 1996.

From 1999 onwards, Piper-Heidsieck became one of the more dynamic Champagne brands in the sector, introducing a rejuvenated visual identity, the red label and new marketing concepts in the fields of “lifestyle products” and “champagne services.”

Jean-Paul Gaultier’s first Piper-Heidsieck bottle design appeared in 1999, an unusually bold and innovative concept within the traditionally conservative world of Champagne.

Around 2001, Champagne and spirits packaging also began undergoing a major transformation, with the appearance of reusable presentation cases, ice bags, cool boxes, 2-in-1 packs, transport cases and other related accessories. This was the period when packaging increasingly began to evolve into independent design and functional objects in its own right.

The rigid rectangular case is covered in a highly textured red woven-effect material. The front features a large embroidered PIPER HEIDSIECK inscription in a matching red tone.

The red body is framed at the top and bottom by beige/gold-toned edging. The sturdy, naturally patinated metal fittings and clasps further reinforce its period appearance.

The lid is secured at the front by two metal clip fasteners. A strong folding carrying handle is fitted on the top, while an additional metal handle on the side makes transport easier. The original metal hinges remain in place on the back.

The interior is practical and thoughtfully designed. The entire inside is lined in black and includes a removable upper tray with its own carrying handle.

The upper tray is designed to hold 2 Champagne glasses. Beneath it is a compartment intended for one full-size 0.75 L Champagne bottle.

The upper section can be completely removed, providing easy access to the lower storage compartment. Its construction shows that this was not simply a presentation box, but a genuinely functional and portable Champagne set.

The piece is in well-preserved vintage condition.

The red exterior covering and embroidered PIPER HEIDSIECK lettering remain in good condition. The beige/gold-toned edging shows some minor scratches, surface marks and wear from use.

The metal clasps, handles, rivets and other fittings show natural patina and some discolouration consistent with age.

The black interior lining and removable tray are also well preserved.

It has been used and shows minor scratches and age-related signs of use when viewed closely.

Bottle and glasses are not included.

A rarely seen Piper-Heidsieck promotional and functional accessory, suitable for a Champagne collection, bar, restaurant or home-bar display.

With its distinctive red Piper-Heidsieck branding, original embroidered lettering, patinated metal fittings and purpose-built interior for one bottle and two glasses, it is an interesting piece for collectors of Champagne accessories and period French advertising memorabilia.
