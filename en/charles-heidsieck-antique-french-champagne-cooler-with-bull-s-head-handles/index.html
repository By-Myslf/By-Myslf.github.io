About this piece

CHAMPAGNE – Charles Heidsieck – REIMS

Charles Heidsieck – Antique French Champagne Cooler with Bull Head Handles and Nose Rings – ARGIT Paris, 1930s–1940s

Measuring 20 × 20 cm, the cooler is made of copper and was originally silver-plated. Over the decades, much of the silver plating has worn away, revealing the warm golden tones of the copper beneath. The remaining silver plating and natural patina give the piece its present appearance.

The most distinctive features are the two elaborately sculpted bull heads on either side, each with a large movable metal ring passing through its nose. These rings serve as both functional handles and decorative elements. The carefully modelled horns, eyes and textured hair demonstrate the detailed craftsmanship of French decorative metalwork of the period.

The upper section is decorated with cast pewter ornaments depicting grape leaves, bunches of grapes and winding vines. These motifs directly reference champagne production and the vineyards of the Champagne region.

The oval metal plaque on the front features the raised inscription:

CHAMPAGNE – Charles Heidsieck – REIMS

The underside bears the original French manufacturer’s mark:

The Charles Heidsieck champagne house was founded in Reims in 1851. During the early 20th century, branded champagne coolers were not only practical accessories for serving champagne but also important promotional objects. They were used in restaurants, hotels and champagne bars, where they helped establish the identity of individual champagne houses.

The Parisian manufacturer ARGIT produced branded metal champagne coolers for several French champagne houses. The inscription Modèle Déposé indicates a registered design.

The bull head handles with rings through their noses are an unusual alternative to conventional ring handles. Their sculptural design, combined with the detailed grapevine decorations, reflects the influence of French decorative arts from the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

The flowing, natural shapes of the grape leaves recall the Art Nouveau style, while the cooler’s simple cylindrical body also fits the aesthetic of the Art Deco period.

The worn silver plating, exposed copper and natural patina are part of the object’s history. Preserving the original surface rather than having it re-silvered helps retain its authentic character.

This nearly century-old champagne cooler is suitable for collectors of vintage champagne accessories, as well as for display in champagne bars, restaurants or home bars.
