About this piece

Manufactured in France from durable, high-density recyclable polyethylene, the cooler features Taittinger’s iconic embossed pattern on one side and the large vertical TAITTINGER logo on the other. Integrated multi-colour LED lighting transforms the cooler into an eye-catching display, creating an elegant atmosphere for receptions, tastings, restaurants, terraces or garden parties.

The generous interior comfortably accommodates 6–8 standard champagne bottles with ice, while the removable bottle support keeps the bottles upright and allows meltwater to drain beneath them. An integrated side drainage tap makes emptying the cooler quick and convenient after use.

The built-in rechargeable lithium battery powers the LED illumination for approximately 5 hours on a single charge. Using the supplied remote control you can easily select fixed colours, automatic colour transitions, flashing mode, white light, pause the colour sequence or adjust brightness to perfectly match the occasion.

Designed for both indoor and outdoor use (IP65), the cooler is suitable for terraces, poolside entertaining, yacht parties, wine events, champagne houses and luxury hospitality venues. When connected to the charger, it can also operate continuously as an illuminated display piece.

The cooler is presented in excellent condition, complete with:

  • Original Taittinger technical manual
  • Original remote control
  • Integrated rechargeable LED lighting system
  • Built-in drainage tap with removable access cover
  • Removable internal bottle support

An increasingly difficult-to-find original Taittinger hospitality display piece that combines functional wine service with illuminated presentation. Perfect for collectors of champagne memorabilia, luxury event professionals, premium restaurants, hotels or anyone seeking an authentic statement piece from one of Champagne’s most prestigious houses.
