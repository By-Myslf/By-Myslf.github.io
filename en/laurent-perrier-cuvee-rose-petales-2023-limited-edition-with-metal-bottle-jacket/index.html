About this piece

The 2023 Pétales design is inspired by flower petals and the various shades associated with rosé champagne.

The 2023 “Pétales” Metal Bottle Jacket

The bottle is enclosed in a two-part, hinged decorative metal jacket that follows the distinctive broad-shouldered shape of the Laurent-Perrier Cuvée Rosé bottle.

The openwork design consists of overlapping floral and petal-shaped patterns in shades of pink, red, orange, burgundy, purple, and blue, framed by rose-gold-coloured metal detailing.

The combination of coloured surfaces and polished metal creates different visual effects depending on the lighting and viewing angle.

The metal jacket can be opened and removed, allowing it to be retained and reused after the champagne has been consumed.

Collectible and Decorative Use

Since 2017, Laurent-Perrier has introduced a series of limited-edition decorative metal jackets for its Cuvée Rosé champagne.

Previous designs include Signature, Constellation, Safari, Butterfly, and Bamboo, followed by the Pétales edition in 2023.

Each edition features a distinctive pattern, making the bottle jackets suitable for individual collecting or as part of a complete Laurent-Perrier Cuvée Rosé collection.

The metal jacket is designed as a reusable decorative accessory rather than disposable packaging.

After removing the bottle, it can be displayed as a decorative object, used as a table centerpiece, or fitted around another compatible Laurent-Perrier Cuvée Rosé bottle.

Its openwork metal construction also makes it suitable for decorative ambient lighting. A small battery-operated LED tealight placed inside the empty jacket allows light to pass through the floral openings, creating patterned light and shadow effects.

This makes it suitable for table decorations, receptions, private celebrations, champagne presentations, and hospitality settings.

If a traditional candle is used, a suitable heat-resistant holder and precautions against overheating are essential. LED tealights are the safer option.

About the Champagne – Laurent-Perrier Cuvée Rosé Brut

Founded in 1812 in Tours-sur-Marne, France, Laurent-Perrier is one of Champagne’s established champagne houses.

The house introduced its Cuvée Rosé in 1968, developing a rosé champagne made exclusively from Pinot Noir grapes using the maceration method.

The grapes are sourced from selected vineyards in the Montagne de Reims area of the Champagne region.

During maceration, which lasts approximately 48–72 hours, the grape skins release their natural colour pigments and aromatic compounds, contributing to the champagne’s characteristic colour and fruit expression.

The champagne undergoes approximately five years of ageing before release.

Aroma and Tasting Profile

Laurent-Perrier Cuvée Rosé displays colours ranging from raspberry pink to salmon-pink tones.

Its aromatic profile is characterised by fresh red berries, including raspberry, strawberry, redcurrant, and black cherry.

On the palate, it offers fresh acidity, a pronounced fruit character, and a rounded, balanced structure.

The champagne can be served as an aperitif or paired with fish, marinated seafood, light meat dishes, selected cheeses, and red-fruit desserts.
