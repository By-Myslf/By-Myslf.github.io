About this piece

DEUTZ Champagne Set – Two Vintage Champagne Coolers and 6 DEUTZ Lehmann Glass Opale 16 cl Champagne Glasses

Both champagne coolers were made in France and are marked MADE IN FRANCE on the base. Each features classic ring handles on the sides and decorative horizontal lines around the body.

The champagne coolers are vintage, used pieces, but are in excellent condition considering their age. Upon close inspection, minor surface scratches and signs of previous use are visible.

The set also includes 6 DEUTZ-branded Lehmann Glass Opale 16 cl champagne glasses. Made in France, these machine-made lead-free crystal glasses are designed for tasting champagne and other sparkling wines.

Glass specifications:

Made in France

The glasses have never been used and are still in their original factory packaging.

Set includes:

1 black DEUTZ champagne cooler + 1 silver-coloured DEUTZ champagne cooler + 6 DEUTZ Lehmann Glass Opale 16 cl champagne glasses.
