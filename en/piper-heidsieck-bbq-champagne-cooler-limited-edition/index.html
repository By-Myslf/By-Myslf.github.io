About this piece

Limited-edition Piper-Heidsieck “Bubbles & Grill” multifunctional champagne accessory that can be used as both a portable barbecue grill and a champagne cooler. New and never used, complete with its original rosé-red felt cover.

Made from stainless steel, the piece features an ingenious dual-purpose design. It can be used as a portable BBQ for grilling, while removing the grill insert and filling the container with ice transforms it into a champagne cooler.

Thanks to its compact size and portable construction, it is suitable for terraces, gardens, picnics and outdoor events. The exterior felt cover features Piper-Heidsieck’s distinctive rosé-red colour and also provides a practical solution for storing and transporting the piece.

The Piper-Heidsieck “Bubbles & Grill” concept is an unusual champagne promotional item, combining a champagne cooler and portable barbecue in a single design. Its limited production and dual functionality make it particularly interesting for Piper-Heidsieck collections, champagne bar displays or as a functional collector’s piece.

A functional champagne accessory and an uncommon Piper-Heidsieck collector’s piece.
