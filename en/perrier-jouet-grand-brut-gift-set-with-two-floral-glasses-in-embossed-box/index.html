About this piece

The Presentation Box

One of the most distinctive features of this set is its sophisticated white embossed presentation box, decorated with the signature floral motifs of the Perrier-Jouët Champagne House.

The surface features delicately raised floral patterns, creating an elegant textured finish. The front of the box displays the stylized silhouettes of a champagne bottle and two graceful champagne glasses, with the Perrier-Jouët Grand Brut branding prominently positioned in the centre.

A dark green ribbon handle allows the box to be carried conveniently while perfectly complementing the House’s traditional green-and-white colour scheme.

The reverse of the box features descriptions in both French and English, introducing the history of Perrier-Jouët and the distinctive character of its Grand Brut Champagne.

The packaging also reflects the House’s commitment to sustainability, as highlighted in the information printed on the box.

The Two Exclusive Champagne Glasses

The set includes two elegant, long-stemmed, tulip-shaped champagne glasses.

Their most striking feature is the delicate floral decoration, combining white and pale pink blossoms with gold-coloured outlines and vibrant green leaves.

The floral design reflects the celebrated artistic heritage of Perrier-Jouët, whose nature-inspired aesthetic has become one of the most recognisable symbols of the Champagne House.

The slender stems, gently curved bowls and beautifully detailed floral motifs give the glasses a refined and distinctive appearance.

Beyond their practical use for enjoying champagne, these glasses also make attractive decorative and collectible pieces.

The Champagne – Perrier-Jouët Grand Brut

Founded in 1811 in Épernay, France, Perrier-Jouët is one of the historic Champagne Houses renowned for its elegant, floral style.

Grand Brut is one of the House’s most recognised champagnes, with a heritage dating back to 1846, when Perrier-Jouët introduced an exceptionally lightly dosed cuvée.

Made from a blend of Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Meunier grapes, Perrier-Jouët Grand Brut is a refined and well-balanced champagne.

Its aromatic profile is characterised by delicate floral notes, fresh fruit and subtle hints of buttery brioche and vanilla.

The bottle features an elegant gold-coloured foil capsule, a distinctive oval label and the iconic Perrier-Jouët typography, perfectly complementing the presentation box and matching champagne glasses.
