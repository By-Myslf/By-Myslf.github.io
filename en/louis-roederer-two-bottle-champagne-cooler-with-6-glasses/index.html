About this piece

Louis Roederer Two-Bottle Champagne Cooler with 6 Champagne Glasses

The Louis Roederer cooler features a modern, curved design with a champagne-gold/bronze finish and LOUIS ROEDERER CHAMPAGNE lettering on both sides. Its size allows it to hold and chill two standard 0.75 L champagne bottles with ice.

Cooler dimensions:

The set also includes 6 Lehmann Premium 18 cl champagne glasses from the P. Jamesse Signature Collection. The glasses are made from lead-free crystal and feature an elegant tulip-shaped bowl designed for champagne tasting.

Glass dimensions:

The six glasses are brand new and have never been used. They come with their original Lehmann box. The original box shows some wear from storage and handling, as visible in the photos.

The set includes:

1 Louis Roederer two-bottle champagne cooler

6 Lehmann P. Jamesse Signature champagne glasses

1 original Lehmann box
