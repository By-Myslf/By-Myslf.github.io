About this piece

Kaukoranta spent nearly two decades exploring and tasting different Dom Pérignon vintages. He purchased his first bottle in 2004 and later focused increasingly on older vintages, including Œnothèque releases as well as the later P2 and P3 editions. The book is the result of many years of collecting, tasting and experience in Champagne.

This large-format, 152-page hardcover book in English provides a detailed overview of Dom Pérignon and the development of its different vintages. It contains 280 tasting notes, 598 ratings and 68 large-format bottle photographs. The book measures approximately 24.5 × 34 cm, making it suitable both as a reference work and as part of a Champagne collection or bar display.

The book is accompanied by an illuminated bar display inspired by the iconic shield-shaped Dom Pérignon label. Its green front features the familiar white Dom Pérignon graphics and lettering. When switched on, it produces a strong green light that highlights the label design, particularly in a darker bar or display environment.

The illuminated display is in brand-new condition. It has a built-in electrical system with an on/off switch and operates via a USB connection, making it simple to set up and use immediately.

The book and illuminated Dom Pérignon display make a great combination for a Champagne collection, bar, restaurant, display cabinet or home bar.
