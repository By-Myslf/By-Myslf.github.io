About this piece

This magnificent French champagne cooler bears the name of the historic Champagne Pommery house of Reims, France. Designed to accommodate several bottles of champagne, it combines impressive proportions with exceptionally decorative craftsmanship.

Its distinctive design is inspired by the flowing, organic forms of the Art Nouveau movement. The generously proportioned bowl is adorned with elaborate, raised relief decorations featuring gracefully intertwining vines, stylised leaves and grape clusters. These sculptural motifs extend around the entire exterior, creating an elegant and harmonious decorative composition.

At the centre of the bowl is a prominent rectangular cartouche bearing the embossed inscription “CHAMPAGNE POMMERY – REIMS FRANCE”, surmounted by the historic Pommery coat of arms.

The richly detailed botanical ornamentation, broad, elegantly moulded upper rim and substantial, stepped pedestal base give the piece a striking, almost sculptural presence.

The entire exterior has been professionally repolished to an exceptionally high, almost mirror-like finish, giving the cooler a remarkably fresh, nearly new appearance. The polished surface beautifully enhances the intricate relief decoration, allowing the sculpted details to stand out with exceptional clarity.

The exterior is exceptionally bright and beautifully preserved, with the appearance of a newly finished piece. Natural age-related oxidation and patination of the pewter are visible only on the unpolished interior of the bowl. These authentic traces of age preserve the object’s historic character and provide a pleasing contrast to its brilliant exterior.

The pedestal is hollow and closed underneath by a fitted sheet-metal plate, covered with protective blue felt to prevent scratching furniture or other display surfaces.

The cooler corresponds to a known decorative Pommery model generally attributed to the 1950s–1960s. Comparable examples featuring the same distinctive raised vine decoration and pedestal construction have appeared at international auctions and specialist vintage dealers. The precise manufacturing date and original metalworking workshop have not been established.

With its generous diameter and substantial construction, the cooler was designed for chilling several champagne bottles simultaneously in ice. Beyond its original function, it is an impressive decorative and collectible object, particularly suited to an elegant dining room, private wine cellar, sophisticated bar or distinguished champagne collection.

Associated Accessory

A matching, highly polished pewter drip tray from the same period is also available as a separate lot. It forms an attractive and complementary accompaniment to the champagne cooler and is believed to have been produced contemporaneously with it.

Collector’s Appeal

This impressive Champagne Pommery pewter cooler is a fine example of the meeting between French champagne culture and the decorative metalwork traditions of the 20th century.

Its monumental proportions, substantial weight of almost four kilograms, richly sculpted Art Nouveau-inspired ornamentation, distinctive heraldic Pommery branding and exceptionally brilliant polished finish make it far more than a functional champagne cooler.

An elegant statement piece with strong decorative presence, historical charm and genuine collector appeal — equally suitable for a distinguished champagne collection, luxurious interior or refined entertaining environment.
