About this piece

CHAMPAGNE – LA GRANDE DAME – LOUIS VUITTON – VEUVE CLICQUOT – 2015

VEUVE CLICQUOT LA GRANDE DAME 2015 × LOUIS VUITTON

Exclusive Limited Edition – 75 cl Brut Champagne in Its Original Louis Vuitton Luxury Presentation Case

The Veuve Clicquot La Grande Dame 2015 Louis Vuitton Limited Edition represents a remarkable encounter between the finest traditions of French Champagne-making and luxury craftsmanship.

This prestigious 2015 vintage is presented in a specially designed Louis Vuitton bottle, accompanied by an extraordinary red luxury presentation case created specifically for this edition.

More than an exceptional Champagne, this edition appeals to collectors and enthusiasts who appreciate distinctive luxury objects, exclusive collaborations, and pieces with a rich historical heritage.

The Louis Vuitton Presentation Case – A Tribute to the Art of Luxury Travel

One of the most striking elements of this special edition is its magnificent red presentation case, inspired by the iconic travel trunks that have defined Louis Vuitton’s heritage for generations.

The case features a vibrant red exterior with a finely textured, horizontally grained finish, complemented by elegant gold-coloured embossed decorative details.

The corner motifs, ornamental clasp design, and precisely arranged gold-coloured dotted accents evoke the craftsmanship and distinctive appearance of historic Louis Vuitton travel trunks.

An important detail is that the gold-coloured corner and clasp decorations are not separately attached metal fittings. They are embossed decorative prints integrated into the surface of the case.

A wide, woven red carrying strap completes the design, featuring the LOUIS VUITTON name subtly incorporated into its textured fabric. A small circular gold-coloured stud bearing the Louis Vuitton inscription adds another refined detail.

The presentation case opens in an elegant, book-like manner, revealing an interior that contrasts beautifully with its striking red exterior.

The inside is finished in a warm ivory-cream colour, with a delicate embossed diamond pattern extending across the inner panels. A specially shaped, curved support at the base securely accommodates the Champagne bottle.

The harmony of colours, textures, and carefully considered details reflects the sophisticated world of Louis Vuitton luxury packaging.

Far more than protective packaging, the presentation case is an essential part of the identity and visual appeal of this exceptional edition.

The Champagne – Veuve Clicquot La Grande Dame 2015

At the heart of this remarkable presentation is one of the most prestigious creations of the Veuve Clicquot Champagne House: La Grande Dame 2015 Brut Champagne.

La Grande Dame is the House’s celebrated prestige cuvée, created in honour of the extraordinary Madame Clicquot.

The 2015 vintage is composed of:

  • 90% Pinot Noir
  • 10% Chardonnay

The remarkable dominance of Pinot Noir gives this Champagne its strength, elegance, and distinctive structure, while Chardonnay contributes freshness, finesse, and balance.

La Grande Dame 2015 offers a sophisticated and complex character, with notes of ripe fruit, citrus, delicate florals, and refined minerality.

The Louis Vuitton edition is further distinguished by its exclusive bottle presentation.

The dark, glossy bottle features the distinctive embossed VCP emblem on its shoulder. Its neck is wrapped in textured black foil and finished with an elegant cream-coloured band bearing the Louis Vuitton name.

The beautifully designed ivory label, framed by a fine gold border, brings together the names of both prestigious houses:

CHAMPAGNE – LA GRANDE DAME – LOUIS VUITTON – VEUVE CLICQUOT – 2015

The result is an exceptionally elegant and distinctive appearance, setting this bottle apart from the standard La Grande Dame 2015 presentation.

The Story – When Two French Legends Meet

The story begins more than two centuries ago.

In 1805, at just 27 years of age, Barbe-Nicole Ponsardin, better known as Madame Clicquot, took control of her family’s Champagne business following the death of her husband.

At a time when women rarely held positions of commercial leadership, Madame Clicquot demonstrated extraordinary courage, determination, and innovative vision.

Through her pioneering spirit and relentless pursuit of excellence, she helped transform Veuve Clicquot into one of the world’s most celebrated Champagne houses.

It is in tribute to this remarkable woman that the House named its most prestigious Champagne La Grande Dame – “The Great Lady.”

Several decades later, in 1854, Louis Vuitton established his own business in Paris.

His innovative and meticulously crafted travel trunks soon became symbols of sophisticated travel, exceptional craftsmanship, and French luxury.

Although their stories began in different worlds, both houses were built upon remarkably similar values: innovation, uncompromising quality, respect for tradition, and an extraordinary attention to detail.

The La Grande Dame 2015 Louis Vuitton special edition brings these two remarkable legacies together.

The Champagne celebrates the spirit and achievements of Madame Clicquot, while the presentation case pays tribute to Louis Vuitton’s heritage of exceptional travel trunks.

An extraordinary Champagne accompanied by an equally extraordinary presentation.

Exclusivity and Collectible Appeal

The collaboration between Louis Vuitton and Veuve Clicquot for La Grande Dame 2015 was already documented as an exclusive special edition in 2023.

According to contemporary specialist reports, the Louis Vuitton version was not intended for conventional Champagne retail distribution. Instead, access to the edition was associated with direct enquiries to the two luxury houses.

The exact number of examples produced has not been publicly confirmed through a verifiable official source.

Its distinctive design, association with two internationally renowned luxury brands, and specially created presentation case make this edition particularly appealing to collectors of prestigious Champagnes and exceptional Louis Vuitton creations.

An Exceptional Piece Beyond the World of Champagne

The Veuve Clicquot La Grande Dame 2015 Louis Vuitton Limited Edition brings together the excellence of French Champagne-making and the distinguished design heritage of Louis Vuitton.

Its appeal lies not only in the prestigious Champagne itself, but also in its exclusive presentation, the fascinating histories of the two houses, and the remarkable original luxury case.

It is a distinguished addition to a carefully curated Champagne collection, an exceptional piece for a Louis Vuitton enthusiast, or a memorable gift for someone who appreciates the rare and extraordinary.
