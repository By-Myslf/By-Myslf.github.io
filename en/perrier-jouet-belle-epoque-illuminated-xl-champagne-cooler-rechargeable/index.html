About this piece

This champagne cooler features an elegant, wave-shaped design, with the Perrier-Jouët Champagne logo and lettering on both sides and the Maison’s signature white anemone floral decorations at both ends.

The integrated LED lighting illuminates the logos and floral motifs, creating an attractive visual effect, particularly in the evening.

Thanks to its built-in rechargeable battery, the cooler can be used completely cordless after charging. It can be moved and positioned anywhere without requiring a continuous power connection.

Bottle capacity:

  • 4–5 standard champagne bottles (0.75 L)
  • 2–3 magnum champagne bottles (1.5 L)

Suitable for champagne collections, champagne bars, restaurants, events, and home bars.
