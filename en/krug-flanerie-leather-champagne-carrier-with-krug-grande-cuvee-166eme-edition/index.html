About this piece

The two pieces are offered together as a collector’s set.

KRUG Flânerie – Portable Champagne Cooler

The KRUG Flânerie was introduced by Maison KRUG in 2012. Crafted from genuine leather, it was designed to carry a single champagne bottle.

Its woven structure, made from light beige leather straps, surrounds a black insulated inner bottle holder. This design helps maintain the temperature of a pre-chilled champagne bottle for approximately two hours, making it suitable for outdoor occasions.

The carrier features an adjustable leather shoulder strap, metal buckles, and KRUG branding.

The collector’s set includes:

  • KRUG Flânerie leather champagne carrier with insulated interior
  • Original adjustable KRUG leather shoulder strap
  • KRUG-branded leather bottle-neck tag
  • Original KRUG Flânerie information booklet
  • One bottle of KRUG Grande Cuvée 166ème Édition Brut Champagne, 0.75 L

The champagne included in this collector’s set is Maison KRUG’s Grande Cuvée 166ème Édition, created around the 2010 harvest.

The 166ème Édition is a blend of 140 different wines from 13 different years. The oldest wine dates back to 1998, while the youngest comes from the 2010 harvest.

Champagne composition:

  • 45% Pinot Noir
  • 39% Chardonnay
  • 16% Meunier
  • 42% reserve wines from previous years

The bottle features KRUG’s traditional gold label and gold foil.

Bottle details:

Together, the 2012 KRUG Flânerie carrier and the KRUG Grande Cuvée 166ème Édition form a collector’s set that reflects both Maison KRUG’s champagne-making heritage and the design of its branded accessories.

The champagne carrier and bottle are offered exclusively as a complete collector’s set.
