Bollinger Champagne Vintage Metal Ice Bucket

SoldRef. CC-39

A charming 1980s vintage French champagne bucket from the world-famous Bollinger champagne house – a special-edition registered model designed by Eric Berthès. It is made of brushed stainless steel with shiny vertical stripes and the Bollinger name in black. The inside is red and shows more signs of use than the outside. Net weight: 897 g.
