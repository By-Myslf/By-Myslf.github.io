Selling a piece or a collection?

I buy vintage champagne-related items and complete collections.

From a single coupe to a lifetime's collection, I'm always glad to hear about objects connected with champagne and its history. Every offer gets a personal reply.

What I'm looking for

See the collection

  • Champagne glasses

    Coupes, flutes and complete services, from pressed glass to engraved crystal.

  • Ice buckets and coolers

    Silver, silver plate, pewter and enamel, with or without stands.

  • Stoppers, taps and openers

    Clamp stoppers, champagne taps, cork pliers and cases.

  • Muselet caps

    Single rare caps, albums and entire collections.

  • Labels and ephemera

    Labels, menus, price lists, posters and advertising.

  • Champagne house items

    Trays, ashtrays, signs and gifts made for a champagne house.

How it works

  1. Send photos

    A few clear photos, the size, any marks or names, and what you know about the history.

  2. Get a reply

    I answer every message, usually within a few days, with questions or an offer.

  3. Agree and hand over

    Payment on collection in Budapest, or by arrangement with insured shipping.

Complete collections

If you are selling a whole collection, or clearing a house or an estate, I'm happy to look at everything together and make one offer for the lot. Collections stay together wherever possible, and their history is recorded in the catalogue.

Everything you send is treated in confidence.
