Bottle coaster with a pierced gallery

Sample text: replace this with your own description.

A low, round stand for a bottle on the dining table, so that drips don’t mark the cloth. The gallery is pierced with a row of arched windows; the base is a disc of turned walnut with a small silver boss in the middle.

Coasters like this were often made in pairs, so a matching one may be out there somewhere.
