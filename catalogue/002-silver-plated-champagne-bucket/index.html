Silver-plated champagne bucket with loop handles

Item type
Ice buckets
Brand
Maison Exemple
Date
c. 1910
Origin
Probably France
Materials
Silver-plated copper
Dimensions
Height 21 cm, diameter 23 cm

Sample text: replace this with your own description.

A plain, heavy bucket with a beaded rim, two turned lines on the body and a stepped foot. The loop handles are fixed with round bosses on either side.

Large enough for a single bottle and plenty of ice. Some of the plating has worn through on the handles, showing the copper underneath.
