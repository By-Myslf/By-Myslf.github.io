Sample text: replace this with your own description.

The small metal disc on top of a champagne cork, held in place by the wire cage, is called a plaque de muselet. Producers decorate them with their initials, a coat of arms or a date, and collecting them has its own name: placomusophilia.

These nine came together in a single envelope at a market in Épernay.
